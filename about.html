ABOUT US

Five students. One real client.
Zero shortcuts.

Delta is a team of five Computer Science students at ISMT College, University of Sunderland. We formed in January 2026 to build SafeMotion — a real, working training platform for a real logistics client. We do not cut corners.

OUR STORY

From module brief to working software

Delta was formed in the first week of January 2026 as part of the CET257 Enterprise Project module at ISMT College. From the very first meeting we agreed to treat this as a real commercial project — not just a student assignment.

We made contact with our client, Mr. Ramji Subedi of the Logistics and Warehousing Company (Automotive Sector), in the first week and held our initial requirements meeting via Microsoft Teams on 31st January 2026. Every interaction with the client was documented formally with written records and email confirmations.

The result is SafeMotion — a fully working, interactive manual handling training platform that addresses every one of our client's confirmed requirements. The prototype was demonstrated to the client on 5th March 2026 and received a score of 23 out of 25 on the assessment feedback form.

"

We treat every client interaction as if we were a professional software house. Formal meetings, documented requirements, tested deliverables and honest communication.

— Nabin Acharya, Project Manager

OUR MISSION

Delta delivers innovative, accessible and cost-effective digital training solutions for logistics and warehousing organisations — built to professional standards and delivered at zero ongoing licence cost.
