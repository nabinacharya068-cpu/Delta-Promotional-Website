LIVE PROJECT — 2025/2026

SafeMotion

Interactive Manual Handling Risk & Training Platform

Client: Logistics & Warehousing Company (Automotive Sector)

Contact: Mr. Ramji Subedi

Role: Health & Safety Training Manager

Status: Delivered — March 2026

SafeMotion is a fully working web-based interactive training platform that replaces face-to-face classroom manual handling training with an accessible, self-paced digital alternative. Staff at the logistics company can complete their legally required annual training at any time, on any device with a browser, without needing to attend a scheduled classroom session.

The system delivers a structured four-step training journey: an introduction to manual handling principles, a 360-degree virtual automotive warehouse exploration, an interactive hazard identification exercise with six clickable hazard markers, and a timed ten-question scenario-based quiz assessment. On passing with a score of 70% or above, staff receive an automatically generated digital completion certificate.
