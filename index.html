SafeMotion — Interactive Manual Handling Risk & Training Platform

Client: Logistics & Warehousing Company (Automotive Sector)
Contact: Mr. Ramji Subedi — Health & Safety Training Manager

SafeMotion replaces expensive classroom training with a fully digital, interactive platform that warehouse staff can access at any time, on any device. The system includes a 360-degree virtual warehouse environment, a hazard identification exercise, a timed 10-question quiz and an automatically generated digital completion certificate.

HOW WE WORK

Professional standards.
Student team.

Delta treats every project as a real commercial engagement. We conduct formal client meetings with written records, maintain version-controlled repositories, test rigorously before claiming completion, and maintain consistent professional communication throughout every stage.

01 Accessibility by Default

WCAG 2.1 AA is our minimum standard on every interface we deliver.

02 Transparency with Clients

We confirm requirements in writing, document every decision and provide written follow-up after every meeting.

03 Zero Unnecessary Cost

We use open-source tools exclusively. Our solutions carry no ongoing licence cost for the client.

04 Test Before You Claim

Nothing is marked complete until it has been tested against defined acceptance criteria by our dedicated tester.
